# The Garbage Man

The Garbage Man is a 3D top-down roguelike Unity game made for a Game Programming final project.

## Story

After a long war between humans and artificial intelligence, the world is filled with dangerous junkyards and abandoned machines. Bob, a poor scavenger known as a Garbage Man, enters these areas to collect valuable materials and earn enough money to buy a house in the city.

## Controls

- WASD: Move
- Mouse: Aim
- Left Click: Shoot
- E: Interact
- Esc: Pause / Skip Intro
